Left mouse button pushes, right one pulls. Inflow is the speed at the inlet; whatever falls over the edge comes back in there. sub is the number of physics steps per frame. When it turns orange, the maximum is no longer enough and the simulation becomes inaccurate. Smoothing is the filter radius in particle spacings: at 0 you see the individual spheres.
