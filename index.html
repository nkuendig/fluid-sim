fluid-sim

A stream of 65,536 particles, computed entirely on the GPU. SPH fluid simulation in WebGPU, a single HTML file with no dependencies.

Start the simulation

Needs WebGPU: Chrome or Edge 144+, Firefox 141+, Safari 26+. The stream starts empty and fills up in about eight seconds.

What you see

  • Water flows from an inlet through a sloped channel, around three stones, and falls over the edge at the end.
  • Every particle that falls out at the bottom comes back in at the top. The particle count stays fixed.
  • The left mouse button pushes the water away, the right one pulls it in. The sliders change gravity, pressure, viscosity and inflow.
  • The “View” button switches between the smoothed water surface and the individual particles.

How it is computed

  • Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics in 3D, every step a compute shader in WGSL.
  • Neighbour search on a grid with a counting sort; the particle data is reordered in memory every step.
  • The stream bed is a signed distance field and doubles as the collision geometry.
  • The water surface is built in screen space: particle depth, bilateral filter, normals from depth.
  • A second version of the same physics on the CPU checks the GPU step by step.
